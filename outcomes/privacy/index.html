Privacy Policy

Who We Are

This privacy policy applies to Outcomes, which is operated by Sliver Craftworks, LLC. It covers the app on iOS and iPadOS.

What Data We Collect

Information users provide directly may include:

  • Installation ID
  • which is unique to an installation. A new one is created every time the app is installed

Information collected automatically through the app or related services may include:

  • Usage and interaction data about how the app or related services are used

Additional data sent off the device may include:

  • Installation ID

Some data leaves the device to support app functionality such as accounts, syncing, purchases, support, or other online features.

How We Use Data

We use your data in a variety of ways, including:

  • To understand product usage and improve the app.
  • To manage billing-related access and entitlements.

The following providers are used:

  • Analytics: TelemetryDeck. Used for product analytics and feature usage.

Additional feature details:

  • Calendars / Reminders: The user may choose to have Outcomes read data from their Calendar and/or Reminders.

When Data is Shared

When an app feature needs online processing, syncing, account handling, or support, relevant data may be sent off the device to operate that feature.

Some data may be shared with outside recipients such as TelemetryDeck when that is needed to operate the app’s features.

The following providers are used:

  • Analytics: TelemetryDeck. Used for product analytics and feature usage. Optional for users.

The only data sent off the device is anonymized and used for analytics only.

Payments and Subscriptions

The app offers in-app purchases or subscriptions through StoreKit.

Data Retention

We keep data only as long as it is reasonably needed to provide the feature, support the account, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices and Rights

Some permissions are optional and are only used if the user chooses the feature that needs them.

Requests can be handled by email.
